email from Christian Liebel at 2026-05-15 (archive)
Dear DAS chairs,

In our review of the draft charter, we mentioned:
We're concerned by the appearance of Web Serial in the Tentative Deliverables. At least Mozilla seems inclined to start implementing that API, and we want it to live in a WG that all implementers are comfortable joining, to ensure that all of their potential concerns about engine/platform capabilities, privacy, and security can be easily raised. That said, its presence in this charter doesn't prevent it from being adopted by another WG instead.

In order to address this point, we acknowledge that the Charter now states for Web Bluetooth, Web Serial, and WebUSB that:
Note: This work may turn into a joint deliverable with the Web Applications Working Group.

However, it is unclear if Web Apps WG have agreed to those becoming joint deliverables. I’ve cc’ed the Web Apps Chairs so that both groups can reach consensus on items that involve them.

Thank you.

email from Marcos Caceres at 2026-05-16 (archive)
Speaking personally (all hats off), I think a new group would be ideal...  preferably in the W3C, but WHATWG is of course totally fine. That would give us all the opportunity to put in place a new/tailored working mode, get all the participants in the same place, and hopefully find someone amazing to chair it. It would take a lot of pressure off both DAS and WebApps and allow us to finish the large set of deliverables our respective groups currently have. We could potentially even move the joint DAS/WebApps deliverables into such a group, which could resolve a lot of conflicts… particularly the “editors shouldn’t be chairs” issue which, admittedly, is a big problem for both DAS and WebApps… we need a great project manager to oversee this stuff, who can steer these complicated deliverables into becoming standard in a reasonable timeframe, and with the proper attention and through consensus building. 
Anyway, I’m excited to see where the WHATWG SG discussions go. But, I’m somewhat concerned that the deliverables are simultaneously listed at the WHATWG and W3C: It feels unfair to be hedging the W3C/DAS as a fallback, which will be confusing when the DAS Charter goes to the AC if these deliverables are listed in the DAS Charter. The members will be voting on a charter that might never receive these deliverables and that feels disingenuous to me personally - even if that’s not the intention. At a minimum, when the DAS Charter does go to the AC with the proposed deliverables, the W3C Membership must be made fully aware that the WHATWG might actually take them. 
You’d need to recharter DAS regardless if DAS does eventually take them, I if my understanding is correct. Listing them in the DAS Charter now feels very rushed and like a bit of a land grab. Specially because these weren’t in the charter when wide review happened a few months ago, so neither the TAG, SING, and the other horizontal groups that reviewed the initial charter have had a chance to weigh in. That kinda invalidates the wide review assessments given the pretty massive scope change that comes with listing these additional deliverables, IMO. 
If possible, we should see how the WHATWG discussions pan out first, and if it doesn’t get off the ground over there for whatever reason, then we have a proper discussion about how to do the work here at the W3C in a new group with a new chair(s) and working mode tailored specifically for these deliverables.
